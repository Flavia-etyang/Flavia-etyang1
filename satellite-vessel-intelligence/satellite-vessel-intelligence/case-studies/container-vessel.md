## Observation

The deck presents repetitive rectangular cargo geometry arranged in
regular bays, a deck crane, and an aft-positioned superstructure.

## Inference

Regular rectangular deck geometry may indicate stacked containerized
cargo. This is consistent with — but does not alone confirm — a
container-vessel classification, since regular deck geometry can, in
some configurations, resemble other cargo arrangements at low
resolution.

## Supporting structural evidence

1. **Container stacks** — visually regular, box-shaped units arranged in
   rows across the deck.
2. **Repetitive cargo geometry** — bay spacing is consistent and
   repeats along the hull's length.
3. **Cargo cranes** — where present, support a containerized-cargo
   hypothesis, though many container vessels are geared for shore-side
   handling and carry no cranes.
4. **Superstructure** — aft-positioned, consistent with common container
   vessel general arrangement.
5. **Hull proportions** — the observed L/B ratio is higher than the
   tanker and LPG cases, consistent with the narrower, longer hull forms
   common to container vessels.
